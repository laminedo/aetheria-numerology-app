TRAINING LEVEL GUIDE
BEGINNER

Focus on form and establishing cardiovascular base. Work-to-rest ratio is typically 1:2 (e.g. 10s work, 20s rest) or 1:1.

INTERMEDIATE

Classic 20s work / 10s rest (Izumi Tabata) and 30/15 protocols. Pushes aerobic and anaerobic threshold with 8+ rounds.

ADVANCED / PRO

High work capacity protocols (40s work / 20s rest, 3-minute championship boxing rounds) demanding maximal VO2 max power.
